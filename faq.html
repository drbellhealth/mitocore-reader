Mitocore FAQ

Quick answers to the questions visitors most often ask about Ortho Molecular Mitocore.

What is Mitocore actually for?

Energy and fatigue. It's a daily formula a practitioner usually hands you for low energy, brain fog, or a generally low feeling after illness or in midlife. A clinician walks through who it helps.

How do I take it?

Three capsules a day. With food. Morning works best — late afternoon will mess with your sleep.

When will I feel it?

Not the first week. Most people who notice anything notice it around week four to six. Some don't notice anything; that's also normal.

What are the side effects?

GI upset if you take it on an empty stomach. Wired feeling if you're sensitive to B-vitamins. Vivid dreams if you take it too late in the day.

Is it safe with my medications?

Some real interactions to know about — anticoagulants, methotrexate, diabetes meds, nitroglycerin. Have your prescriber look at the list.

Pregnant or breastfeeding?

Switch to a prenatal-specific multivitamin. Talk to your provider.

Any allergens?

Free of the eight big ones. The NAC may be a problem if you're sulfur-sensitive.

Where do I learn more?

Read the practitioner review.
