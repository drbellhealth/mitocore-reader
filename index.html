What is Mitocore?

Mitocore is a daily multivitamin from Ortho Molecular Products. The ingredients are aimed at supporting the cellular energy-production system. Three capsules a day, taken with food. Practitioners hand it out for fatigue, brain fog, and post-illness energy decline. It's not a stimulant — it's slow-acting and works over weeks.
