Reader-First Editorial

If you got here from a Google search after a chiropractor or integrative-medicine clinician handed you a bottle of Mitocore, this site exists for you. The editorial bias is toward short answers, plain English, and a clinician's review one click away for the deeper read. We try to neither oversell nor dismiss the formula.
